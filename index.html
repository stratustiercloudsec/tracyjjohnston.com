About Tracy

For more than 15 years, I've led the programs that modernize how money moves and how organizations use their data. My work sits between strategy and delivery: I take a business goal, turn it into a roadmap engineering teams can build, and keep everyone who has a stake in it moving together until it ships and people use it.

I started by managing the modernization of the Federal Judiciary's CM/ECF platform, the system federal courts use to manage cases and accept filings. From there I consulted for Safe-Guard Products and Elavon, spent six years at US Bank/Elavon modernizing digital payments and co-leading its enterprise Agile transformation, and today lead data, AI and platform programs at Global Payments.

The thread through all of it is regulated, high-stakes work. I bring systems thinking and analytical rigor to messy programs, I build risk and compliance into delivery from day one, and I tie every decision back to an outcome the business can measure.

Federal Judiciary, with Avaya Government Solutions

Modernizing the federal courts' electronic filing system

The situation
CM/ECF is the system the U.S. federal courts use to manage cases and accept electronic filings. Its Next Generation modernization involved judges, clerks, IT teams and vendors across multiple phases.
What I did
Managed the modernization across technical, operational and stakeholder teams, and translated what judges and clerks needed day to day into development priorities.
The result
100+ stakeholders coordinated through implementation, and legacy data and workflows migrated across 10+ federal court systems.
