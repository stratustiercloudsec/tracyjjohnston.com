About Tracy

I lead enterprise technology transformations, the work of changing not only the technology an organization runs on, but how it plans, decides, operates and delivers value.

My work sits between strategy and execution. I take complex priorities leadership cares about, translate them into executable transformation, and bring together the people, platforms, governance and decisions needed to move from ambition to measurable outcomes.

Over 15+ years, I’ve applied that capability across government, financial services, payments, fintech and mission-driven organizations. My experience has progressed from federal technology and legacy modernization to enterprise Agile transformation, Transformation Management Office leadership, and today, enterprise data, AI and platform modernization. Along the way, I’ve led major government technology initiatives, supported nonprofit technology transformation, scaled new operating and delivery models, and advised senior executives on complex strategic initiatives.

That breadth gives me a ‘systems’ view of transforming across organizational boundaries and stakeholders to align and move together. I understand how strategy, technology, data, architecture, security, governance, finance, people and organizational change intersect and where transformation can break down when those pieces are managed independently.

As data and AI reshape how organizations operate, compete and serve their customers and communities, I’m increasingly focused on helping organizations turn emerging technology into scalable enterprise capability. That means creating clarity from complexity, building governance and accountability into execution, connecting investment to value, and ensuring transformation produces outcomes leaders can measure and organizations can sustain.

Three stories

What this looks like in practice, in government and the private sector.

Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts, with Avaya Government Solutions

Modernizing a federal electronic filing system

The situation
CM/ECF is the system the U.S. federal courts use to manage cases and accept electronic filings. Its Next Generation modernization involved judges, clerks, IT teams and vendors across multiple phases.
What I did
Managed the modernization across technical, operational and stakeholder teams, and translated what judges and clerks needed day to day into development priorities.
The result
100+ stakeholders coordinated through implementation, and legacy data and workflows migrated for 94 district courts across 50 states.
